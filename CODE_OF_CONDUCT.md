# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security › Report a vulnerability** form (mark it "Code of conduct"), or use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.
